# Netflix Content Strategy Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix’s content catalog to uncover **strategic patterns in content production, distribution, and audience targeting** using data visualization.

The goal is not just to describe the data, but to **extract insights that can influence content investment decisions**.

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### 1. Aggressive Expansion Phase (Post-2015)

Netflix rapidly scaled its content library after 2015, peaking around 2019.
This reflects a **market capture strategy driven by original content investment**.

### 2. Movies Dominate, But TV Shows Drive Retention

Movies form the majority of the catalog, indicating:

* Faster production cycles
* Lower commitment for viewers

However, the steady rise in TV shows suggests a shift toward **long-term engagement and user retention**.

### 3. Heavy Geographic Concentration

Content production is dominated by:

* United States
* India
* United Kingdom

This indicates **over-reliance on a few regions**, leaving room for expansion into underserved markets.

### 4. Standardized Movie Duration

Most movies fall between **90–120 minutes**, showing:

* Strong alignment with viewer attention span
* Optimization for completion rates

### 5. Content is Targeted Toward Mature Audiences

Ratings such as **TV-MA and TV-14 dominate**, indicating:

* Focus on adult viewers
* Underrepresentation of family-friendly content

### 6. Seasonal Release Strategy

Content additions peak in certain months (e.g., mid-year, holidays), suggesting:

* **Strategic release timing aligned with user demand cycles**

---

## 🧠 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

* Increase investment in **TV shows** to improve retention
* Expand production in **emerging international markets**
* Maintain optimized movie duration (90–120 mins)
* Diversify into **family and younger audience segments**
* Align content releases with **high-engagement seasons**
